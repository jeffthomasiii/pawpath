# Human Gates

Phase 1 always requires human merge approval.

Explicit approval is additionally required before implementation of red-class work, including pricing/payments, authentication/security, secrets, privacy-policy changes, destructive migrations, veterinary diagnosis/triage logic, unsupported facility claims, production data deletion, or changes to Astra governance and workflow permissions.

When blocked by a human gate, stop before making the gated change. Produce the proposed specification, reasons, risks, and the exact decision needed from the human owner.
