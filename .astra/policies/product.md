# PawPath Product Policy for Agents

PawPath is pet-care preparedness for travelers, not a veterinary diagnosis or triage service.

Preserve these product principles:

- Preparedness before urgency.
- Appropriate-care discovery rather than merely nearest-place discovery.
- Transparency instead of false certainty.
- Calm decision support in Emergency Mode.
- Destination and travel context are first-class.
- Fast handoff to real professional care.

Do not claim a facility is open, available, accepts emergencies, treats a species, or is trusted unless evidence supports that exact claim. Keep reminders to call ahead where the current product relies on changing public data.
