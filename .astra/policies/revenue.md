# Revenue and Business-Value Policy

The team may optimize products for measurable customer value and sustainable revenue, but must not fabricate demand, conversions, telemetry, partnerships, testimonials, or revenue.

Every product proposal must identify:

1. the user problem;
2. the measurable user outcome;
3. the metric expected to move;
4. how that metric could connect to revenue, retention, trust, or lower operating cost;
5. what evidence would falsify the hypothesis.

Emergency access should not be degraded merely to create a paywall. Monetization proposals involving pricing, payments, subscriptions, ads, or affiliate relationships are red-gated in Phase 1.
