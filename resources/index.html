Travel with confidence

Useful resources for the road.

Preparation, official travel guidance, and trusted places to start when your pet needs care away from home.

Before you go

Travel-ready checklist

  • ID + records: microchip/contact info, vaccinations and regular-vet details.
  • Each pet: medications, allergies and concise care notes.
  • Road kit: water, food, bowls, leash/carrier, waste supplies and first aid.
  • Care plan: confirm Primary + Backup hours, services and species accepted.

Away from home

Reduce decisions under stress

  • Call before driving. Confirm the facility can see your pet and provide the needed service.
  • Keep your regular vet reachable. They may be able to provide records or context.
  • Use veterinary professionals for medical guidance. PawPath organizes preparedness and navigation; it does not diagnose or triage.
