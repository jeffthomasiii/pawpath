Useful resources for the road.

Before you go

Pack the information you may need.

Identification + records

Confirm microchip/contact information, vaccinations, medications, allergies, your regular veterinarian's contact information, and any travel documents before leaving.

Road-trip basics

Bring water, food, bowls, leash or carrier, waste supplies, medications, and a pet first-aid kit. Secure pets appropriately in the vehicle and plan regular stops.

Care plan

Save a Primary and Backup near your destination, then call both to confirm hours, services, species accepted, and what to do after hours.

When something changes

Reduce decisions under stress.

Call before driving

Facility listings can be incomplete or stale. Confirm the facility is open, can see your pet, and can provide the service you need whenever circumstances allow.

Keep your regular vet reachable

Your home veterinarian may be able to provide records or context to the treating facility while you are away.

Use veterinary professionals for medical guidance

PawPath organizes preparedness and navigation. It does not diagnose, triage, or replace veterinary care.
