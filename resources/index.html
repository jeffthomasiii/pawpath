Preparedness resources

Know what to confirm before you go.

PawPath helps organize care options; it does not diagnose, triage, or guarantee facility availability.

Pet adventure imagery · Unsplash

Before the trip

Call your Primary and Backup

Confirm hours, services, species accepted, after-hours procedures, and whether the facility expects you to call before arriving.

Keep essentials accessible

Have your veterinarian's contact information, medications, allergies, and other concise information you may need while traveling.

When you need care

Call ahead when possible

Online listings and OpenStreetMap data may be incomplete. A phone call is the best way to confirm current availability and capabilities.

Use professional guidance

PawPath is a preparedness and navigation tool, not veterinary diagnosis or medical triage. Contact a veterinary professional for medical guidance.
